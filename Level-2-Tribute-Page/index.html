Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Explore His Journey

About the Legend

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He was widely known as the Missile Man of India for his contribution to India's missile and space programmes.

He inspired millions of students to dream big, work hard and contribute to the development of the nation.

Life Journey

1931

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1954

Graduated in Physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.

1960

Joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO).

1980

Contributed to the successful launch of the Rohini satellite.

1997

Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.

2002

Became the 11th President of India.

2015

Passed away on 27 July while delivering a lecture in Shillong.

Major Achievements

Missile Development

Played an important role in India's missile development programmes.

Space Research

Contributed to India's space research and satellite launch programmes.

Bharat Ratna

Received India's highest civilian honour in 1997.

People's President

Connected with students and encouraged young minds across India.
